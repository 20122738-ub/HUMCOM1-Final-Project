Compose and send your first email

Beginner · About 12 minutes · Computer demonstration + practice

Before you begin

  • A computer with an internet connection
  • Your own email account and sign-in details
Use your own device or sign out when you finish on a shared computer. Never share your password.

Follow along

Your email, step by step.

These steps work in most email services. Button names may look slightly different on your screen.

1. Open your email and choose Compose

Sign in to your own email account. Find Compose, New message or New email. A blank message window will open.

2. Add a recipient in the To field

For practice, send the message to yourself. Type your own email address carefully and check every letter.

3. Write a helpful subject line

Click Subject and type “My first practice email”. A short, clear subject helps the recipient understand your message.

4. Write your message

Add a greeting, one or two sentences and your name. Do not include passwords or sensitive information.

5. Check the details, then choose Send

Read your message again. Check the To address and subject. When everything looks right, select Send once.

6. Find your message in Sent

Open Sent or Sent items to confirm the email was sent. If you sent it to yourself, check your Inbox too.

Not ready to send? Close the message and look in Drafts. You can return to it later.

Now it is your turn

Try a safe practice email.

Create your message

Use a clear subject and write two sentences about something you learned.

Send it to yourself

Check your own address, choose Send and look for the message in your Inbox.

Check your work

Can you find it in Sent? Open the email and reply with “Practice complete”.
