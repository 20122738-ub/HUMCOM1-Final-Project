Why TechWise exists

A friendly place to learn the essentials.

TechWise is a digital literacy training website for people who are new to computers or want a fresh start. It brings clear lessons, demonstrations, practice activities and safety guidance into one place—so you can build skills you will actually use at home, in learning and at work.

Our mission

Make digital skills feel achievable.

Give beginners clear, jargon-free guidance and useful practice, helping them become more independent with devices and online tasks.

Our vision

An everyday digital world for everyone.

A future where people can use technology with confidence, make safer choices and keep learning—regardless of their starting point.

Small steps, practical skills

A learning program that moves at your pace.

Six beginner modules take you from device basics to staying safe online. Choose a topic, replay a lesson and practice when you feel ready.

1

Learn something useful

Short lessons and captioned demonstrations explain one task at a time, with guides to keep.

2

Try it for yourself

3

See your confidence grow

Review completed lessons, revisit tricky topics and earn a completion certificate for each finished module.

More than knowing which button to press

Skills that make a difference, every day.

Digital literacy means knowing how to use technology, understand information and act safely—not knowing everything.

Handle everyday tasks

Book an appointment, fill in a form or find opening hours without relying on someone else.

Stay connected

Send an email, join a video call and share moments with the people who matter to you.

Make informed choices

Recognize suspicious messages, check information and decide what you feel comfortable sharing.

Built for a comfortable start

Clear language, readable layouts, captions and printable materials. Take breaks, repeat lessons and ask for help whenever you need it.
